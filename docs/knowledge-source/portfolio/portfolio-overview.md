# KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio overview

## What products does KnowMe AI make?

KnowMe AI, LLC and Prometheus AGS make a portfolio of products, grouped on this page in six sections: Applications, Agents and memory, Platform and infrastructure, Developer tools and skills, Integrations, and Services and research. Those six names are this page's own. The Boss, the free open-source desktop workspace, has a published release. So do the Prometheus Skill Pack and Prometheus Skills Mini.[^portfolio] KnowMe, the personal AI platform, is listed as implementation and specifications, and the portfolio records no release for it.[^portfolio] The portfolio index itself filters the products into eight categories: Applications, Capabilities, Client & partner work, Infrastructure, Integrations, Research & concepts, Services and Tools.[^portfolio]

## How the portfolio is grouped

The portfolio is the published list of products and projects from KnowMe AI, LLC and Prometheus AGS.[^portfolio] The status of each product is the portfolio's own wording. It is not an independent audit.[^portfolio]

## What is available, in development or design only

Seven products record a published release or package: The Boss, Universal Agent Runtime, Flint Forge, Prometheus Entity Management, Prometheus Parking Lot, Prometheus Skill Pack and Prometheus Skills Mini. IPFS Sync for Obsidian has a pre-release. Each has its own caveat below.[^portfolio] Many others are existing source with no release. Some are prototypes. Many are designs or specifications only, with no implementation.[^portfolio] Version lines are the latest recorded when the portfolio was checked on 24 October 2026.[^portfolio]
