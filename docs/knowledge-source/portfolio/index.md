# KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio

Scope: concept files that give the whole portfolio at a glance: which products there are and how the portfolio describes their status, which relationships the sources state, and what can be used today.

## Concepts

* [Portfolio overview](portfolio-overview.md) - Every product in the portfolio, grouped in six families, with its status in the portfolio's own words and its version.
* [Stated relationships](portfolio-stated-relationships.md) - The relationships between products that the portfolio or a repository states, each with its source, and the pairs the sources do not relate.
* [How the products relate](portfolio-how-products-relate.md) - The few relationships the sources state most clearly, with the portfolio's framing that they are architectural intent.
* [What can I use today](portfolio-available-today.md) - The products with a published release or package, and the ones that are not available to use yet.
