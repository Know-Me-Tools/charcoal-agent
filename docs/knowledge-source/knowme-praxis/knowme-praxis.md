# KnowMe Praxis and the TripTych lineage status and what is stated

KnowMe Praxis is an agentic container and system architecture specification. The historical TripTych source is kept as its lineage. The portfolio lists Praxis as published specifications and says runtime completion is not inferred, so Praxis is a specification, not available software.[^portfolio]

## KnowMe Praxis status and version

The portfolio status for KnowMe Praxis is "Published specifications; runtime completion not inferred."[^portfolio] No repository was identified for this entry, so there is no version number to quote. The specification editions are numbered: see below.

## What is stated about KnowMe Praxis

- The portfolio lists the TripTych original source, Praxis 1.0 and 1.1 as historical, and Praxis 1.2 as the full specification.[^portfolio]
- It also lists a lightweight Praxis 1.2 share entry and a stable entry that always points to the latest edition.[^portfolio]
- It counts 239 related parts and source-record editions under Praxis.[^portfolio]
- The share entry and the full 1.2 specification are separate artifacts, and preview behaviour in messaging clients is not fully verified.[^portfolio]

## How KnowMe Praxis relates to other products

The portfolio lists the Private Dot (Locus) documents among the published Praxis source records.[^portfolio] The sources do not state any other relationship. The portfolio treats relationships among its entries as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
