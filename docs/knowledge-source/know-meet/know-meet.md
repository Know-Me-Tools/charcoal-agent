# Know Meet status and what is stated

Know Meet is an agentic meeting experience that brings together personal assistants, communications, calendar and meeting artifacts. The portfolio lists it as a 70-screen/state synthetic prototype, with operational integrations unverified. It is a prototype, not a meeting service you can use.[^portfolio]

## Know Meet status and version

The portfolio status for Know Meet is "70-screen/state synthetic prototype; operational integrations unverified."[^portfolio] Its repository is Know-Me-Tools/know-meet. Version line: {{v:meet}}.

## What Know Meet is stated to do

- The repository README describes Know Meet as a LiveKit video meeting where people, their persistent dots and registered LiveKit agents meet around one shared memory.[^meet-readme]
- The README points to a hosted prototype and brand kit (it labels them live), and to a design system, logo and screens under docs/design.[^meet-readme]
- The prototype screens listed are sign in, meetings dashboard, meeting, agents, recordings, settings and invitation.[^portfolio]
- Phone, SMS, meetings and recordings in the prototype are simulated.[^portfolio]
- A separate conferencing specification is private.[^portfolio]

## How Know Meet relates to other products

The README names persistent dots as meeting participants.[^meet-readme] The portfolio says Know Meet integrates personal assistants, communications and calendar, and that these integrations are unverified.[^portfolio] The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
