# Numen Enclave status and what is stated

Numen Enclave is a reusable architecture for central coordination with local authority. The portfolio lists it as a published architecture and deployment standard, version 0.1: a review proposal dated 23 October and published 24 October 2026. Implementation and operational acceptance remain to be demonstrated, so Numen Enclave is a proposal.[^portfolio]

## Numen Enclave status and version

The portfolio status is "Published architecture and deployment standard v0.1: a review proposal dated 23 October and published 24 October 2026."[^portfolio] No repository was identified for this entry, so the only version number is the standard's own, 0.1.

## What Numen Enclave is stated to do

- The owner domain keeps private data, inference, workflows, approvals and durable receipts.[^portfolio]
- A narrowly scoped management plane handles identity, connectivity and approved operational metadata.[^portfolio]
- The portfolio also lists a concrete proposed deployment profile.[^portfolio]
- "Enclave" denotes a governance and execution boundary, not a hardware TEE or regulatory certification.[^portfolio]
- Identity and network metadata still require minimization and review.[^portfolio]

## How Numen Enclave relates to other products

The portfolio says Numen Enclave adds a proposed deployment boundary to the Numen execution fabric.[^portfolio] The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
