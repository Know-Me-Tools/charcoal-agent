## Checks the generator enforces on this copy

`node scripts/site/build-directory.mjs --check` fails on: a public row with no entry; an entry for an id that is not in the facts file, or is not public; a summary over 90 characters; a question that does not end in "?" or is over 70 characters (the index prompt too); a multi-row tile without a summary; a row in two tiles; any of "seamless", "cutting-edge", "leverage", "utilize", "synergy", "revolutionary", "unleash", "supercharge"; a price, "GDPR", "HIPAA", "SOC 2", "certified", "compliant", "guarantee"; any of the facts file's `withheldTerms` (Headscale, Mac mini, RTX 4070, VRAM); and the name of any row that is not public.

## Places where the summary leans on the row, for the content officer

- `uar`: label Released (a v1.2.3 release exists) but the portfolio says "Work stopped; current runtime qualification not established". The summary states only the row's own sentence ("shared runtime dependency used by multiple portfolio applications") and says nothing of present activity.
- `forge` (Released) and `fabric` (In development): the rows call their README claims "a source claim". The summaries describe what the components are, not that they are production ready.
- `demos` is labelled In development by the generator (rule S7: scaffold exists), while the summary says "Proposed hosting", as the row's summary does. Both are true to the row; a visitor sees a "proposed" service under an "In development" label.
- `knowme`: the row's summary opens with "Private memory". The summary drops "private" and says "personal intelligence app" with its label In development; the landing page still says KnowMe is not yet released.
- `companion`, `meet`, `documind`, `flint`, `realtor`: "prototype" is in the summary because the row's `portfolioStatus` says prototype, synthetic or MVP.
- `concierge`: "The chat client behind this site" describes this site's own client (the row: "KnowMe web and desktop client").

## Decisions for the operator

1. **"Ask about the products." section kept.** The brief proposes removing it (section 3.2, "PROPOSED REMOVAL, for the content officer and the operator"). That text is operator-approved (`landing-site.ts`, `site-landing.md`) and the brief leaves the decision open, so it is untouched. With it, the sections below the directory alternate canvas/band as before (products on band); without it, "Your conversations are stored in this browser." would sit on the band, as the brief draws. Its body names three products and is now stale beside a directory of 52.
2. **Version line wording follows the brief, not the knowledge-base build.** The brief (section 2.4) and the appendix give "No release · last commit 20 Oct 2026" and "No release recorded". `scripts/kb/lib/product-facts.mjs` renders "no tagged release (last commit YYYY-MM-DD)" for the knowledge base. The generator follows the appendix, because its drift check is defined against it.
3. **Multi-row tiles have one ask chip**, as drawn in the brief. The three Flint rows and the two Skill Pack rows each show their own badge and version, and share the tile's question.
4. **No "See all products" link and no tile links yet.** `/products` and `/products/<slug>` ship in the next change.
