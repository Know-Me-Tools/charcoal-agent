Concept A — The Anchor

The Anchor

A thick, filled K built from smooth rounded shapes — not stroked outlines but solid mass. The stem is a substantial rounded rectangle, and the arms are bold tapered wedges with softened ends. The ember is larger here, confident, sitting at the convergence point like a cornerstone. No frame needed — the weight of the letterform is the frame. This mark owns every pixel it occupies.

→ Heavy. Grounded. Unmistakable at any size.

Concept C — The Monolith

The Monolith

The K is carved out of a solid filled squircle — a bold mass with the letterform revealed as negative space. The ember sits inside the knockout as a glowing dot, the only color against the monochrome block. This is the most app-icon-ready concept. It reads as a single confident shape from across a room. The smooth knockout curves give it warmth despite the mass.

→ A monolith. One solid object. Unmissable.

Concept D — Conviction

Conviction

The synthesis. A bold filled K with smooth organic curves, contained in a weighted squircle frame. The stem and arms are solid shapes with rounded terminals — substantial enough to feel powerful, curved enough to feel personal. The ember is prominent: a 10px circle at the heart with a subtle warm glow. The frame stroke is thick enough to feel present but doesn't compete with the letterform. This is confident self-knowledge made visible.

→ This is the one. Bold conviction with a warm center.
