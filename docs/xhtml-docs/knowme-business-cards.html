Business Card Designs

Four distinct approaches to the KnowMe business card — each using the Conviction logomark and full brand token system. Standard 3.5 × 2 inch proportions at print-ready specification.

01 · The Architect

Structural precision. A vertical ember rule anchors the left edge like a datum line on a blueprint. Subtle grid lines cross the card surface at key alignment points. The logomark sits top-right, grounding the composition. Information flows down the left column with clear hierarchy: name in Space Grotesk, title in Inter, contact in JetBrains Mono.

→ Feels like a specification sheet. Engineered, not designed.

02 · Ember Edge

A bold 6px ember strip runs the full left edge of the card — your brand signature visible the moment it leaves a cardholder. Name dominates the upper left at 20px, the KnowMe lockup sits bottom-right as an anchor. Contact details are clean Inter, not monospaced — this is the most approachable variation.

→ The edge catches the eye first. Then the name. Then the brand.

03 · Monolith

Brand-dominant layout. The "KnowMe" wordmark leads at top-left with ember accent on "Me." A large ghosted K watermark fills the right half at near-invisible opacity. Personal info sits below a thin divider. Contact details run along the bottom in a compact mono row. This card says the brand first, the person second.

→ The brand is the structure. You are the signal within it.

04 · Centered

Perfect symmetry. Everything stacks on a vertical axis — icon, name, title, ember divider, contact. A thin ember accent crowns the top edge. This is the most personal variation: your identity at the center of the frame, balanced and unhurried. Best for one-on-one introductions where the card lingers in the hand.

→ Symmetry communicates confidence. The center holds.
