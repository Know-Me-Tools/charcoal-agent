# Know Meet status and what is stated

Know Meet is an agentic meeting experience that brings together personal assistants, communications, calendar and meeting artifacts. The portfolio lists it as a 70-screen/state synthetic prototype, with operational integrations unverified. It is a prototype, not a meeting service you can use.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## Know Meet status and version

The portfolio status for Know Meet is "70-screen/state synthetic prototype; operational integrations unverified." Its repository is Know-Me-Tools/know-meet. Version line: no tagged release (last commit 2026-10-19).

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What Know Meet is stated to do

- The repository README describes Know Meet as a LiveKit video meeting where people, their persistent dots and registered LiveKit agents meet around one shared memory.
- The README points to a hosted prototype and brand kit (it labels them live), and to a design system, logo and screens under docs/design.
- The prototype screens listed are sign in, meetings dashboard, meeting, agents, recordings, settings and invitation.
- Phone, SMS, meetings and recordings in the prototype are simulated.
- A separate conferencing specification is private.

Source: Know Meet repository README, retrieved 2026-10-24.
Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## How Know Meet relates to other products

The README names persistent dots as meeting participants. The portfolio says Know Meet integrates personal assistants, communications and calendar, and that these integrations are unverified. The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
