# OpenAI dots and KnowMe (Landscape)

Visitors ask how KnowMe relates to OpenAI dots, so this page describes dots as OpenAI's own pages did on 2026-10-12 and then compares them with KnowMe. Dots are a shipped product from OpenAI; KnowMe is not publicly released, so every statement about KnowMe here is KnowMe AI, LLC's design intent. OpenAI's facts change often; check OpenAI's pages for the current state.

## What OpenAI dots are (Landscape)

According to OpenAI's announcement, dots are always-on agents. Each dot has its own cloud computer and browser, is powered by GPT-6 Astra, works toward goals around the clock, learns from feedback, and connects through plugins to more than 4,000 apps. A dot can message the person with progress and questions, and when idle it does proactive research using read-only access to connected apps.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## How a person reaches an OpenAI dot (Landscape)

According to OpenAI's announcement, a person reaches a dot through ChatGPT on desktop, web and mobile, in Slack and Teams, and by voice call; texting is described as coming. OpenAI's help page says a dot is set up in the ChatGPT desktop app or desktop web; mobile apps can talk to an existing dot but cannot create one, and dots are not supported on mobile web.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.
Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## Who OpenAI dots are for (Landscape)

According to OpenAI's announcement, dots were rolling out in ChatGPT to Pro and Business Premium plans in eligible markets, with an Enterprise option (including Edu and Healthcare) that an administrator enables. OpenAI also previews specialist dots with their own identity and credentials for organisations, and describes a Microsoft Agent 365 integration as in progress.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## Where OpenAI dots are available (Landscape)

OpenAI's help page says Pro availability excludes the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the UK; Business Premium is available across supported regions; Enterprise is off until an administrator enables it.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## What OpenAI dots cost (Landscape)

Pricing for OpenAI dots is by OpenAI plan and is out of scope for this knowledge base, which states no amounts for any product. OpenAI's announcement says the first dot is included in an eligible plan and says more dots, and more capacity per dot, will be available in future. For current prices and plan terms, see OpenAI's own pages. Pricing has not been announced for KnowMe.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## How OpenAI dots use memory (Landscape)

According to OpenAI's help page, a dot receives memories from ChatGPT and creates its own, including from connected apps, and carries context across the channels it is reached on. Disconnecting an app does not delete information the dot already obtained from it. Deleting a dot's saved data requires deleting (resetting) the dot, which removes its conversations, memories and scheduled tasks.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## How OpenAI says dot data is used for training (Landscape)

OpenAI's announcement says content from ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces is not used to improve models by default, and that on personal plans the person controls this. OpenAI also says it does not train directly on proactive research or a dot's notes to itself.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## How OpenAI dots handle permissions and control (Landscape)

According to OpenAI, the person chooses which apps a dot may access. Custom Rules give four behaviours for a kind of action: act without asking, act if pre-approved, ask before acting, or hand off to the person. Certain sensitive actions, for example changing a password, always stay with the person. An Activity View shows what the dot is doing, a monitoring system can pause or stop a dot, and the person can pause and resume it.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.
Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## Where an OpenAI dot runs (Landscape)

OpenAI's help page says a dot has its own cloud computer, separate from the person's own computer; access to the person's local computer is optional and off by default, and the person can take over the dot's cloud browser.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## Stated limits of OpenAI dots at launch (Landscape)

OpenAI's help page lists limits at launch: a dot cannot place calls to the person, has no standalone email address, and texting is a limited US-only Pro beta through a third-party provider. The same page states that a dot can make mistakes, including when following rules. Beyond these pages, this knowledge base makes no claims about dots' reliability, security or performance.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## How KnowMe differs from OpenAI dots, by design intent (Landscape)

Where a dot runs on OpenAI's cloud computer with OpenAI's model and keeps its memories in OpenAI's systems, KnowMe is designed to run on the person's own devices, to keep conversations, memory and settings in an encrypted local database there, to sync only between the person's own devices and servers, and to work without a central account. Where a dot is tied to one vendor's model, KnowMe is designed to route work through the Universal Agent Runtime so that local models or several providers can serve the same tasks. Its assistants and agents are meant to adapt to the person from a knowledge graph on the person's devices. These are design statements from this knowledge base's KnowMe pages; KnowMe has not shipped.

## What OpenAI dots offer that KnowMe does not today (Landscape)

- Dots exist and can be used now on eligible plans; KnowMe has no public release and no announced date.
- Dots work around the clock on OpenAI's servers while the person's devices are off; KnowMe is designed to run on the person's own hardware, so nothing runs when that hardware is off unless the person operates a server of their own.
- OpenAI cites plugins to more than 4,000 apps and reach into Slack, Teams and voice; KnowMe has announced no comparable integrations or channels.
- KnowMe's scheduled agents (Hands), multi-device sync and plugin system are designed but not built, and a dot comes with OpenAI's infrastructure where KnowMe asks the person to provide models, hardware, storage and sync.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## What KnowMe is designed to offer that OpenAI's pages do not describe for dots: data and training (Landscape)

These are design differences, not claims that KnowMe performs better. OpenAI's pages describe data and memories held in OpenAI's systems, deleted by resetting the dot, with training controls that depend on the plan; KnowMe is designed to keep data on the person's device, where deletion is the person's own act and no training on the person's data is designed in.

Source: OpenAI Help Center, Getting started with your dot, retrieved 2026-10-12.

## What KnowMe is designed to offer that OpenAI's pages do not describe for dots: models, plans and licence (Landscape)

OpenAI's pages tie dots to GPT-6 Astra and to OpenAI plans with regional limits; KnowMe is designed for model choice across local and several hosted providers and is planned for release under the MIT licence with no central account. Until KnowMe ships, these remain intentions.

Source: OpenAI, Introducing dots (2026-09-29), retrieved 2026-10-12.

## Which fits whom, OpenAI dots or KnowMe (Landscape)

Choose OpenAI dots if you want an always-on agent today, you are on an eligible OpenAI plan in a supported region, and you are comfortable with your agent's memory living in OpenAI's systems under OpenAI's controls. KnowMe is being designed for people who want an assistant that adapts to them while their data stays on hardware they control and who do not want to depend on one model vendor; it asks for patience, because it is not released, and for more hands-on operation. In the meantime, KnowMe AI, LLC's downloadable product is The Boss, a free, open-source desktop workspace for macOS and Windows, which is a client for models and agents rather than an always-on agent.

Source: The Boss download page, retrieved 2026-10-12.
