# Companion (provisional working name) status and what is stated

Companion is a provisional working name for a potential shared coordination workspace for dot, Locus and other agent systems. The portfolio records a tested local Axum and Postgres MVP. The broader product remains proposed, and Companion is not available.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## Companion status and version

The portfolio status for Companion, paraphrased on the cloud point, is: "Tested local Axum/Postgres MVP with task hierarchy, events, deduplication, SSE replay and five MCP tools. Prototype screenshots exist. Cloud deployment is not verified." No repository was identified for this entry, so there is no version number to quote.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What Companion is proposed to do

- Companion is proposed to show progress centrally, surface approvals and blockers, track deliverable receipts and coordinate work across agents.
- The tested MVP runs locally.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What remains ahead for Companion

- No live cross-agent integration, recurring reporting pipeline or public standalone publication is verified.
- The portfolio says "local MVP tests do not establish cloud readiness."
- The working name is provisional.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## How Companion relates to other products

The portfolio describes Companion as a potential workspace for dot, Locus and other agent systems, and says no live cross-agent integration is verified. The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.
