# The Operator Service (business model) status and what is stated

The Operator Service is the planned packaging, onboarding, tiers and operating model for persistent AI-assisted work. The portfolio lists it as a service and economics design. A commercial launch is not established, so the Operator Service is not on offer.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## The Operator Service status and version

The portfolio status for the Operator Service is "Service and economics design; commercial launch not established." No repository was identified for this entry, so there is no version number to quote. The sources give no pricing.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What the Operator Service is stated to include

- The Operator Service is planned as a way to package persistent AI-assisted work as a service, with onboarding and tiers.
- The portfolio lists Operator Model v1 (14 October), an earlier economics source, a companion operator-model source and a full operator architecture paper.
- Several local business-model editions exist without verified publication addresses.
- The portfolio does not reproduce commercial terms.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## How the Operator Service relates to other products

The sources do not state a relationship between the Operator Service and other KnowMe products beyond the shared theme of persistent operation. The portfolio treats relationships among its entries as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
