# KnowMe dot operator service status and what is stated

The KnowMe dot operator service is a proposed persistent shared control plane with a canonical per-user memory and task-scoped retrieval for replaceable workers. The portfolio lists it as a specification and synthetic prototype, with no live service certified. It is not a service you can use today.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## KnowMe dot status and version

The portfolio status for KnowMe dot is "Specification and synthetic prototype; no live service certified." No repository was identified for this entry, so there is no version number to quote.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What KnowMe dot is stated to do

- KnowMe dot is proposed as a persistent shared control plane for replaceable workers.
- It is proposed to hold one canonical memory per user and to give workers retrieval limited to the task at hand.
- The portfolio lists a full technical operator paper (version 3, 19 October) and a condensed synthetic architecture preview.
- Per-user brain additions remain unpublished. The preview behaviour is synthetic and "does not certify service capabilities."

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## How KnowMe dot relates to other products

The Know Meet repository describes a meeting room where people, their persistent dots and registered agents meet around one shared memory. The portfolio describes Companion as a potential shared workspace for dot, Locus and other agent systems. The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.

Source: Know Meet repository README, retrieved 2026-10-24.
