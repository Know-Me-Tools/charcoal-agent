## What the Rust performance engineering skill states

- It has six modules: workflow evidence, runtime ownership, versioned streaming adapters, database use, scheduler selection and source provenance.
- An optional helper checks aggregate PostgreSQL connection budgets.
- The public download includes the skill, design and usage source, reproducible helper tests and explicit validation limits. Private reviews and internal fixtures are excluded.
