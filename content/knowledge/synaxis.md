# Synaxis by KnowMe status and what is stated

Synaxis by KnowMe is a proposed harness-independent durable messaging, orchestration and shared memory service. The portfolio lists it as a published design. An operating paid service is not established, so Synaxis is not available.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## Synaxis status and version

The portfolio status for Synaxis is "Published design; operating paid service not established." Synaxis is a working name. No repository was identified for this entry, so there is no version number to quote.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What Synaxis is stated to do

- Synaxis is proposed as a durable messaging, orchestration and shared memory service.
- It is proposed to work independently of any one agent harness.
- The portfolio lists one design document, dated 21 October, and one edition record.
- The portfolio records no independent qualification of a service implementation.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

The sources do not state a relationship between Synaxis and other KnowMe products. The portfolio treats relationships among its entries as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.
