# Numen Enclave status and what is stated

Numen Enclave is a reusable architecture for central coordination with local authority. The portfolio lists it as a published architecture and deployment standard, version 0.1: a review proposal dated 23 October and published 24 October 2026. Implementation and operational acceptance remain to be demonstrated, so Numen Enclave is a proposal.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## Numen Enclave status and version

The portfolio status is "Published architecture and deployment standard v0.1: a review proposal dated 23 October and published 24 October 2026." No repository was identified for this entry, so the only version number is the standard's own, 0.1.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## What Numen Enclave is stated to do

- The owner domain keeps private data, inference, workflows, approvals and durable receipts.
- A narrowly scoped management plane handles identity, connectivity and approved operational metadata.
- "Enclave" denotes a governance and execution boundary, not a hardware TEE or regulatory certification.
- Identity and network metadata still require minimization and review.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.

## How Numen Enclave relates to other products

The portfolio says Numen Enclave adds a proposed deployment boundary to the Numen execution fabric. The portfolio treats such relationships as architectural intent, not proof of completed integration.

Source: KnowMe and Prometheus portfolio, Edition 2, retrieved 2026-10-24.
