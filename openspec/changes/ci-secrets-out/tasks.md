- [x] 1.1 Remove `UAR_JWT_SECRET`, `UAR_SETTINGS_ADMIN_KEY` and `SURREALDB_ROOT_PASSWORD` from `.github/workflows/site.yml`: the "Mask secrets in logs" and "Apply Secrets (idempotent)" steps (lines 104-128) stop writing `uar-secrets` and `surrealdb-auth`, and the seed step (line 167) no longer receives `UAR_JWT_SECRET`.
  Both steps removed outright (not just the three keys — `uar-secrets` and `surrealdb-auth` are no longer written by this workflow at all, including the `QWEN_TOKEN_PLAN_API_KEY`/`DASHSCOPE_API_KEY` entries they used to carry into `uar-secrets`; those move to the operator's one-time creation in 1.2). The seed step's env no longer includes `UAR_JWT_SECRET`. `grep -rnE 'UAR_JWT_SECRET|UAR_SETTINGS_ADMIN_KEY|SURREALDB_ROOT_PASSWORD' .github/workflows/` returns nothing (see 1.6 evidence).
  **Known, tracked gap:** `scripts/seed-site-agent.sh` still only knows how to mint its own HS256 JWT from `UAR_JWT_SECRET` (task 1.3 below replaces this, and is still open pending `gate-site-credentials`). With this task done and 1.3 not yet done, the seed step in `deploy` will fail (the script dies with a clear "`UAR_JWT_SECRET` must be set" message, not a silent bad auth) until 1.3 lands. This is the explicit, expected sequencing of this change and `github-deploy-workflows`'s dependency on it — not a regression introduced here.
